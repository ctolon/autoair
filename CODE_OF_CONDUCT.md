# Code of conduct

AutoAir follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and any other channel where the project is represented.

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus
on what is best for the community. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and
publishing others' private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, **@ctolon**, using the contact
details on https://github.com/ctolon. Do not report it in a public issue. Reports are
reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
correction, warning, temporary ban and permanent ban, depending on the impact of the
behaviour.
